ReelSlider - Seek, Speed, Auto-Scroll, Download & Volume Controls for Instagram
=========================================
Instagram gives you no way to seek, skip, slow down, auto-scroll, or download a Reel — ReelSlider fixes that. Add a real seek bar, playback speed control, media downloading, auto-scroll, multi-language support, and volume memory to every Reel, Story, Feed post, and video on Instagram.

Whether you want auto-scroll through Reels hands-free, speed up a slow Reel, download a carousel of high-quality photos, slow down a fast talker, or skip ahead on a long Story, ReelSlider gives Instagram the same controls YouTube and Netflix already have — completely free, with no account and no data collection.

✦ WHY PEOPLE INSTALL REELSLIDER
-----------------------------------------
• Hands-Free Watching? Auto-scroll Reels automatically advances to the next Reel when the current video ends (ON by default, toggleable via popup or 'G' key).
• Can't seek or skip on Instagram Reels? Click or drag the timeline to jump anywhere in the video.
• Want to save posts and videos? A native download button gets you the highest-quality version of any Reel, video, or photo post directly to your device.
• Want to speed up or slow down an Instagram Reel? Set playback speed anywhere from 0.25x to 2.0x — remembered automatically next time.
• Multi-Language Support? Smart auto-detection matches your Instagram page language (English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Türkçe) with an in-popup selector.
• Tired of Instagram resetting your volume every time you scroll? Set a custom default volume that sticks across sessions.
• Annoyed by sound turning back on as you scroll? Mute Memory keeps your mute/unmute choice as you move between videos.
• Scrolling too much? Set a Daily Instagram Limit to cap your usage, complete with an auto-pause break screen.

✦ KEY FEATURES
-----------------------------------------
• Auto-scroll Reels — automatically scroll to the next Reel when the current video ends (ON by default).
• Seek & Scrub — click or drag the timeline on any Instagram Reel, Story, Feed post, or Profile video.
• One-Click Downloading — instantly download high-quality videos and photo posts straight to your device.
• Playback Speed Control — slow down or speed up Instagram Reels from 0.25x to 2.0x, saved automatically.
• Multi-Language Auto-Detection — smart auto-detection & manual language selector (EN, DE, ES, FR, PT, TR).
• On-Screen Speed UI — see your playback speed in a sleek popup when using keyboard shortcuts.
• Custom Default Volume — set your preferred volume once; it persists across every session.
• Mute Memory — your mute/unmute preference carries over as you scroll Reels.
• Daily Instagram Limit — stay mindful with a customizable daily usage cap (e.g. 1h/day).
• Works Everywhere — Reels, Stories, Feed, Posts, and Profile tabs, all with the same controls.

✦ POWER-USER KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
-----------------------------------------
• [Space] Play / Pause
• [A] or [←] Seek backward 3 seconds
• [D] or [→] Seek forward 3 seconds
• [W] / [S] Increase / Decrease playback speed
• [Shift+W] Hold for temporary 2.0x max speed
• [Shift+S] Hold for temporary 0.5x slow speed
• [M] Mute / Unmute
• [F] Toggle fullscreen
• [G] Toggle auto-scroll (ON / OFF)

✦ 100% FREE & OPEN SOURCE — ZERO DATA COLLECTED
-----------------------------------------
ReelSlider runs entirely in your browser. No data is collected, stored, or sent anywhere. No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no Pro tier — every feature is free, forever. The full source code is public on GitHub.
