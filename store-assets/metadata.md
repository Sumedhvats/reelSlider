### Description
```text
=========================================
ReelSlider - Take full control of your Instagram videos!
=========================================

Instagram doesn't offer native video controls, but ReelSlider adds them seamlessly. Seek, scrub, adjust volume, change playback speed, and loop sections on Reels, Stories, Feed posts, and more!

✦ KEY FEATURES
-----------------------------------------
• Seek & Scrub: Click or drag the timeline on any Instagram video to go to any part.
• Playback Speed: Speed up or slow down videos from 0.25x to 2.0x (remembered automatically).
• Custom Default Volume: Set your preferred default volume level that persists across sessions.
• Mute Memory: Keep your mute/unmute preference as you scroll through Reels.
• Section Looping: Repeat specific parts of any video with custom loop start/end points.
• Works Everywhere: Seamlessly integrated into Reels, Stories, Feed, Posts, and Profile tabs.

✦ POWER-USER KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
-----------------------------------------
• [S] - Play / Pause
• [A] - Seek backward 5 seconds
• [D] - Seek forward 5 seconds
• [M] - Mute / Unmute
• [F] - Toggle fullscreen
• [[] - Set loop start
• []] - Set loop end
• [\] - Clear loop

✦ PRIVACY-FIRST
-----------------------------------------
ReelSlider runs entirely in your browser. Absolutely zero data is collected, stored, or sent to external servers. No accounts required.
```
